## 1. The Complete REI Hiring Guide

**Why hire (the math)**
- 8 working hrs/day; 3 hrs data + 2 hrs admin leaves 3 hrs revenue work -> 62% of day produces zero income.
- Every hour on a $500/mo task is an hour not on a $5,000 activity.
- Three signals it is time to hire:
  - **Time ceiling**: more time on admin (data pulls, skip trace, mail, CRM updates) than revenue work.
  - **Data volume overflow**: lists grow faster than you process; records sit unworked; skip traces pile up; leads go stale because nobody touches them within first 24 hrs.
  - **Deal flow plateau**: leads exist, nobody works them; conversion flat because follow-up drops after first attempt.
- Budget 6 months of the new hire's salary WITHOUT counting on revenue. Can't sustain 6 months without a deal -> cut costs first. "Hiring on hope" kills businesses.

**The Replacement Ladder (6 roles, in order)** — never hire role #3 until #1 and #2 are producing.
1. **Data Manager** — $500-700/mo, Philippines.
   - Duties: upload + clean lists in DataSift CRM; skip tracing batches + Trestle phone scoring; direct mail via Sift Mail; pull first-to-market courthouse data; hygiene (dedup, update statuses, remove sold).
   - KPIs: 200-500 records processed/day; <5% duplicate rate; upload daily for FTM lists, weekly for niche.
   - Hire when: you spend 2+ hrs/day on data; lists unprocessed >48 hrs; consistent deal flow funds 6 months of the role.
2. **Prospector / Caller** — $750-1,000/mo, Latin America, Egypt.
   - Duties: 150+ outbound dials/day minimum; follow niche sequential cadence (3-day cycle); log every outcome in DataSift with correct dispositions; follow-up texts after voicemails per cadence script; escalate motivated leads to Lead Manager immediately.
   - KPIs: 150+ dials/day; 1 correct number per 32 dials; 8-15 meaningful conversations/day.
   - Hire when: Data Manager produces clean skip-traced lists faster than you can call; need more volume than one person; scale trigger 2 Callers per 1 Lead Manager.
3. **Lead Manager** — $1,500-3,000/mo, Latin America (hiring guide) **[conflict: team-structure says US-based or experienced VA]**.
   - Duties: qualify all inbound with 4 Pillars (Reason, Timeline, Condition, Price); follow-up cadence hot every 1-2 days, warm every 15, cold every 45; run STABM daily (Status, Tasks, Board, Messages) before any calls; set + confirm appointments for closers; run not-interested recycling (20-30% of deals come from here).
   - KPIs: 50+ follow-ups/day; 3-5 qualified appointments/week; temperature accuracy (hot/warm/cold matches behavior).
   - Hire when: callers generate more leads than you can follow up; lead response time >1 hr ("400% drop in conversion after 1 minute"); capital-rich but time-poor (W-2) -> compress: hire together with Data Manager.
4. **Closer** — $2,000 base + 3-10% commission, US typically.
   - Duties: convert qualified leads to contracts; comps + deal analysis (ARV, rehab, MAO); 5-10 offers/day; handle four core objections: Price, Time, Third Party, Trust; push ghosted leads back to Lead Manager with temperature preserved.
   - KPIs: 5-10 offers/day; close rate tracked by lead source + temperature; revenue/deal varies by market.
   - Hire when: 2 Lead Managers consistently produce more qualified appointments than you can handle; you're the bottleneck on closing, not lead gen.
5. **Sales Manager** — $5,000-6,000 base + 2-5% override on team closings, US.
   - Duties: train/manage LMs + Closers; monitor team KPIs (dials, follow-ups, appointments, close rates); weekly sales meetings + pipeline reviews; build/refine scripts + objection playbooks; coach or replace underperformers.
   - KPIs: team close rate improving QoQ; each rep covers 3-5x total cost; <15% quarterly turnover.
   - Hire when: sales team >4 people; more time managing people than doing deals.
6. **Operations Manager** — $5,000-7,000 base + performance bonus, US.
   - Duties: all backend systems (CRM, data pipelines, marketing automation); vendors (mail houses, skip trace, phone systems); process compliance; HR/onboarding/terminations; remove owner from day-to-day tactical decisions.
   - KPIs: 95%+ SOPs followed correctly; monthly data-hygiene audit; all integrations/workflows operational.
   - Hire when: team 8+ and systems breaking; you want out of day-to-day entirely.

**Who to hire first, by phase**
- Rules: first hire = Data Manager once deals fund it; overflow triggers each next seat; ~2 Callers per LM; never hire a salesperson before the marketing layer.

- P4 Owner: Sales Manager past 4 sales seats, Ops Manager at 8+ people; deep-prospect every unreached door, instrument KPIs, coach from real recordings; clone into 2nd market only after this one is maxed. Budget $3,500-8,000+/mo.

## 2. Team Structure

- Team = production line, 7 stations; skip one, line stops. Wrong team costs more than no team (panic-hiring burns ~$5,000/mo).
- Weakest link: dirty data wastes callers' minutes on wrong numbers -> fix data first, then add volume.

**Seven roles**
1. **Owner/Acquisitions (you)** — solo workflow: pull FTM courthouse data; 150+ calls (smrtPhone click-to-dial); log leads/dispositions in DataSift; follow up warm/hot; comp + offer; coordinate mail via Sift Mail. Tools: DataSift CRM, smrtPhone, SiftMap, Sift Mail. Evolution: hire DM -> stop data/skip trace/mail; hire Callers -> stop dials; hire LM -> stop qualifying/follow-up; hire Closer -> stop offers/negotiation. Goal: only strategy + high-value relationships remain.
2. **Data Manager** ($500-700, Philippines). Daily:
   - 9:00 pull fresh courthouse records (probate, tax sale, pre-foreclosure, code violations)
   - 10:00 skip trace batches ($0.15/record or $97/mo unlimited; DataSift skip trace, second pass via Skip Genie)
   - 11:00 Trestle scoring: Dial First 81-100, Second 61-80, Third 41-60, Fourth 21-40, Drop 0-20 (tag numbers)
   - 1:00 trigger Sift Mail (handwritten mailers Day 1 of niche cycle; same-day for niche; from CRM filters)
   - 2:00 deep prospecting prep (exhausted-lead lists for L2-L3 research; queue Claude research prompts)
   - 3:00 filter management (move records through sequential funnels; update attempt counts)
   - 4:00 hygiene (flag sold, remove dupes, dead leads) + KPI report to owner
   - Tools: DataSift CRM, Trestle, Sift Mail, Claude, Skip Genie. KPIs: 50+ records/day **[conflict: hiring guide 200-500]**, 95%+ skip accuracy, 0 missed mailings, daily filter updates.
   - 25-100 correct numbers = 1 deal; one deal pays 6-12 months of this role.
3. **Prospector/Caller** ($750-1,000, LatAm: Mexico, Ecuador, Colombia). Daily: 9:00 review list from DM (Dial First at top); 9:15 dial (smrtPhone click-to-dial for niche, ReadyMode power dialer for bulk; work all 3 phone numbers per record); all day 150+ dials, voicemail on EVERY attempt, a DIFFERENT text after each attempt (vary to avoid spam flags); log dials/correct/conversations in Tally Counter; route warm leads to LM immediately (same day); 4:30 disposition cleanup in DataSift (correct, wrong, no answer). KPIs: 150+ dials, 32:1, 100% VM + text rate, same-day lead handoff. At 150 dials ~5 correct numbers/day.
4. **Lead Manager** ($1,500-3,000, US-based or experienced VA; must handle real seller conversations). Bridge marketing -> Acquisitions. Qualifies with 4 Pillars, sets starting temperature, runs cadence. **Every interaction ends with an appointment OR a dated follow-up task — no third option. Does NOT make offers or close.**
   - 9:00 overnight inbound (texts, VM returns, web forms), contact every new lead same day
   - 9:30 hot follow-ups every 2 days **[hiring guide: 1-2 days]**
   - 10:00 qualification calls (4 Pillars + price band set STARTING temperature; situation dictates cadence; a probate 6 months out can still be Hot if neighborhood wanted)
   - 11:00 book appointments with Acquisitions (calendar block with property details + notes)
   - 1:00 warm every 15 days (check if circumstances changed)
   - 2:00 cold every 45 days — cold is active pipeline with manual follow-up, not a drip
   - 3:00 push-back loop: re-contact ghosted leads, temperature preserved, don't restart qualification
   - 4:00 CRM updates; every lead leaves the day with a dated task
   - KPIs: 150 dials/day (floor), 25 conversations/day, 2-5 Sent to AQS/day, 100% same-day contact. Tools: DataSift, smrtPhone.
   - Temperature = cadence, not motivation count. Situation beats pillars: an auction date stays Hot. Healthy qualified pipeline ~75% Cold / 15% Warm / 5% Hot.
   - 20-30% of all platform deals come from not-interested follow-ups.
5. **Closer** ($2,000 + 3-10%, US experienced negotiator). Consultative, not aggressive. Daily: 9:00 review appts; 10-12 seller meetings (reference foreclosure guide, pattern-interrupt openers, no high pressure); 1:00 comps (SiftMap Pro, Market Finder, Two-Bucket method, feature adjustments for condition); 2:00 written offers/contracts, MAO incl. rehab + margin checks; 3:00 negotiation follow-ups, downsell if rejected; 4:00 pipeline review with LMs, identify bottlenecks. KPIs: 5+ offers/week **[conflict: hiring guide 5-10/day]**, 20%+ close rate, $10K+ avg spread, <14 days to close. Ratio 2 LMs : 1 Closer; closer doing 2 deals/mo at $15K spread pays for self + whole team.
6. **Sales Manager** (only at 3+ callers, 2+ closers; $5-6K + 2-5% override). Train/onboard; review recorded calls + coaching; monitor dials, correct numbers, conversations, close rates; weekly accountability meetings; report daily/weekly to owner; SOP adherence. Does NOT close. KPIs: 150+/caller/day, 20%+ team close rate, low turnover, weekly performance review.
7. **Operations Manager** ($5-7K + variable bonus; only with multiple closers, a sales manager, consistent flow; makes sense at 5+ deals/month). Build/maintain SOPs per role; CRM hygiene at scale (thousands/month); integrations/automations (Zapier, sequences, drips); vendors (DataSift, smrtPhone, mail houses); tech budget; project management for rehabs/complex deals. KPIs: 99%+ data accuracy, 0 missed mailings, 100% SOP coverage, monthly tech audit.

**Replacement Ladder (rungs + feelings)** — every hire replaces you on your cheapest task.
- You Today ($0) "Stuck": do everything (pull data, skip trace, 150+ calls, texts/VM, qualify, mail, comps, offers, CRM/filters).
- Rung 1 Admin — DM $500-700 "Stalled": removes 3-4 hrs/day; transfers courthouse pulls, skip trace, Trestle, mail coordination, CRM filter mgmt, hygiene. You keep calls, texts/VM, qualify, comps, offers.
- Rung 2 Marketing — Callers $750-1,000 each "Friction": transfers all outbound (150+/caller), VM + texts, initial appointment setting. You keep qualify/follow-up, comps, offers, strategy.
- Rung 3 Sales — LM + Closer $3,500-5,000+/mo "Freedom": LM takes qualification, cadences, booking, push-back loop; Closer takes negotiation/offers, comps. You keep final approval on large deals, strategy, team performance review.
- Rung 4 Leadership — Sales Mgr + Ops Mgr $15,000-25,000+/mo (total) "Flow": you focus on vision/growth, high-value relationships, market selection/expansion, capital allocation.
- Capital compresses the ladder: W-2 keeps you off phone 9-5 -> hire DM + LM together (one feeds scored lists, other answers calls; without both, one sits idle).

**Who uses what (every tool has an owner; shared logins kill accountability)**
- DataSift CRM: primary DM; also LM, Caller, Owner (database, filters, skip trace, sequences).
- smrtPhone: primary Caller (click-to-dial); LM (inbound), Owner.
- ReadyMode: Caller, bulk campaigns only (multi-line power dialer).
- Smarter Contact: primary DM; Caller for follow-up texts (SMS).
- Sift Mail: DM (in-product handwritten mail).
- Trestle: DM (0-100 phone score).
- Tally Counter: Caller.
- SiftMap / Market Finder: Closer; Owner.
- Claude (Pro): DM (deep prospecting automation).

**Ratios**: 2:1 callers/LM; 2:1 LMs/closer; 150+ dials/caller/day; 2-5 Sent to AQS per LM/day; 32:1 dials/correct; 25-100 correct #s per deal.
**Team scale cost**: solo $0 -> +DM $500-700 -> +2 callers $2,000-2,700 -> +LM $3,500-5,700 -> +Closer $5,500-7,700+ -> +Sales+Ops Mgr $15,000-25,000+/mo.
- Don't hire ahead of your data: if DM isn't producing 50+ leads/day, a second caller won't help.

**Handoffs (what passes)**
- DM -> Callers: scored segmented lists: address, owner name, 3 phones ranked by Trestle, property type tag, attempt count. Mailing list to mail house same day for niche.
- Caller -> LM: contact name, phone, brief notes, initial interest, property details; same day (Harvard: 400% higher conversion within first minute).
- LM -> Closer: 4 Pillars score, seller price expectation, condition notes, starting temperature; appointment or dated task; warm/cold stay on 15/45-day cadence.
- Closer output: signed contracts + feedback upstream (which sources produce closeable deals, which property types convert, recurring objections).

## 3. SOP Creation Guide

- Trap: hiring without SOPs -> 2 weeks live training -> VA leaves at 60 days -> start over. It's a documentation problem.
- Do: build SOPs before posting jobs; first hire reads the system Day 1. Not: train each hire live from scratch.
- DataSift uses Playbook Creator internally for every new process.
- Example: 40-min, 6,200-word rambling transcript -> 11-page SOP: 4-phase process map with decision gates, 6 numbered steps (Goal/Actions/Decision Gates), 2 decision trees, screenshot placeholders, worked example (real Knox County property), quality checklist (6 items), 7 pro tips. Example SOP: "How to Pull Foreclosures for Knox County, TN".

**5-step pipeline**
1. Learn the skill — do the task yourself first (e.g., pull foreclosures at least twice); 1-4 hrs. Mistake: documenting a process you only watched (miss decision points).
2. Record — Loom (or Fireflies for meetings); narrate every click, decision, "if this then that"; 20-60 min. Mistake: rushing.
3. Transcript — Loom auto-transcribes (transcript tab, copy); Fireflies dashboard within minutes; 2 min. Mistake: editing transcript first — give it everything.
4. Feed to Playbook Creator in Claude; 3-5 min. Mistake: short/vague input.
5. Polish + deploy — replace screenshot placeholders with real screenshots; store in Google Drive SOP library by role; share Day 1; 15-30 min. Screenshots cut training questions by half.

**Capture tools**: Loom (primary; free tier; screen + camera; auto-transcription; data pulls, CRM setup, filter config) loom.com; Fireflies.ai (bot joins Meet/Zoom; training calls, Q&A) fireflies.ai; Live training + Teach Claude Chrome extension (records browser actions as structured steps; in-person, phone, browser-heavy). Same process recorded 3 times -> feed all 3.

**Playbook Creator**: download .skill from Google Drive -> claude.ai new conversation -> attach .skill -> Claude confirms -> paste transcript. Inputs: transcripts, written descriptions, existing docs, narration, multiple recordings. Outputs: SOP/playbook, Mermaid process maps (max 7 nodes), decision trees per fork, screenshot placeholders, 5th-grade reading level, pro tips. Requires Claude Pro $20+/mo (no Max needed).

**Anatomy of generated SOP (Knox foreclosure example)**: source summary (6 steps, 2 trees, 3 tools: county website, Google Sheets, DataSift CRM; 7 pro tips; 1 worked example); phases Research -> Access -> Extract -> Upload; decision trees: (1) real foreclosure? check Lis Pendens filing, verify against Register of Deeds, confirm active; (2) property type: Residential -> standard comp; Commercial -> skip; Land -> different valuation. Step format: Goal, Actions, Decision Gates, Screenshot Placeholders, Pro Tips (e.g., Step 3: Document Search, Instrument Type "Lis Pendens", last 90 days). Quality checklist: formatting correct, all fields populated, dupes removed, property status verified, CRM upload confirmed, screenshot of final list saved; plus Common Problems table + printable Quick Reference card.

**SOPs by role**
- Data Manager (Priority #1): county-specific pull (one per county), skip tracing workflow, list upload + tagging, cleaning + dedup, CRM field mapping.
- Cold Caller: opening script by list type, objection flowchart (price, timeline, third party, trust), disposition + status assignment, voicemail framework, follow-up cadence by temperature. Record yourself making 10 calls.
- Lead Manager: STABM daily routine (Status, Task, Board, Message) before outbound; appointment setting scripts; hot/warm/cold cadence; task workflow; CRM status change procedures.
- KPI Tracker: daily sheet entry (admin-updated vs self-reported columns), weekly review prep, caller scoring, expense categorization, monthly trend reporting.

**Library**: naming `[ROLE] - [PROCESS] - [VERSION]` (e.g., "Data Manager - Knox County Foreclosure Pull - v2.1"); top-level "SOPs" Drive folder, one subfolder per role, older versions in "Archive", increment version on change.
**8-step verification ladder**: Loom your #1 daily workflow; feed transcript; add screenshots; create DM SOP folder; record Cold Caller script walkthrough; SOPs for every CRM status change (triggers New->Hot, Warm->Dead); onboarding folder of role SOPs (read Day 1); quarterly SOP review calendar event.

**By phase**: P1 county data pull (per market), marketing cadence setup, lead follow-up workflow. P2 data pull handoff, STABM routine, appointment scripts, status triggers (LM $2,000-3,000/mo needs routine SOP Day 1 **[conflict with $1,500-3,000 elsewhere]**). P3 every role SOP, handoff procedures, onboarding packets, accountability workflows (SOP before job posting; people are replaceable components). P4 genealogy research, county record navigation, heir identification, KPI procedures — this library is what you clone into the next market.
**Better output**: narrate decisions not clicks ("I check Zillow because county site doesn't show renovation status"); include edge cases ("mobile home -> skip, can't finance") -> decision node; 20-min thorough > 40-min unfocused; don't skip screenshots; don't treat first output as final — walk the steps yourself. SOP library = most valuable asset after pipeline.

## 4. KPI Tracking & Team Accountability

- 80% of stalled REI businesses had enough leads — tracking problem (couldn't state cost per contract, dials-to-appointment, which caller produces).
- Track leading indicators (dials, conversations, appointments, offers); not only lagging (revenue, deals, profit — problem started ~90 days earlier).
- Visibility alone raises dial counts 15-20%.
- Do the Company Audit first to find the bottleneck.

**Revenue reverse-engineering calculator**: inputs Monthly Revenue Goal, Average Deal Profit, Close Rate -> outputs Deals/mo (and /week), Offers/mo (/day), Appointments/mo (/day), Conversations/mo (/day), Dials/mo (/day), Contracts target/week.
- Callers needed = monthly dial target / 150 (one caller 150-200 dials/day with power dialer). Scary dial number = need more callers, not motivation.

**Funnel ratios**
- Dials -> correct numbers 32:1 (correct, working, will talk). Trestle cuts the ratio by killing dead numbers pre-dial. Two-way conversations should be 15-20% of dials; <15% = list/number problem, not effort.
- Conversations -> appointment 5:1 (script, speed to lead, training).
- Appointments -> offer 1.5:1 (offer on ~2 of 3 appointments).
- Offers -> contract 4:1 (worse -> check comping accuracy, offer strategy).
- Phone scoring guide claim: 32:1 -> 8-10:1 by scoring before dialing.

**DataSift REI KPI Sheet (Google Sheet, File > Make a copy)** — 10 tabs (older layout): Start Here (company, operator, start date, color codes; 2 min, do first); Goals & Setup (revenue target, avg deal size, close rate, assumptions -> reverse-engineered targets per role); Expenses (tech stack, marketing, team, overhead); Monthly Data (leads, appts, contracts, revenue by month; update at month end vs Goals); Dashboard (green on track/red behind; weekly meeting); Review Dashboard (MoM trends, conversion, cost per lead over time; monthly review); Caller Tracker (dials, talk time, conversations, appts, leads; weekly 5,500+ dials/caller; **Blue columns admin-only from dialer: Dials, Leads, DNC; Yellow self-reported: Time Spent, Campaign** — prevents gaming); Lead Manager (150 dials/day floor, 25 conversations/day, 15-20% conv rate, 2-5 Sent to AQS/day money metric, 100% same-day contact, 0 leads missing tasks; high dials + low AQS = follow-up speed or qualification problem); Transactions (address, purchase, rehab est, ARV, projected profit, status; projected vs actual accuracy); Direct Mail (list, pieces, date, cost, responses, cost/response; mail = third touch at $0.50-2.00/piece; rising cost/response -> check list quality before raising volume).
- New canonical template (ships in DataSift Lead Management plugin): tabs Leads Summary, First to Market, Lead Management, Acquisition, SmrtDialer, Exhausted, Offers Summary, Costs, Expenses; Executive Summary funnel: Leads Generated, Qualified, Appointments, Offers, Accepted, Contracts, Under Contract, Profit. **Scorecards**

## 5. REI Company Audit

- Business moves at the speed of its narrowest pipe; audit finds it. 4 steps: track expenses -> score 20 questions (1-5) -> dashboard (overall, pillar breakdown, radar, bottleneck ranking) -> question-by-question analysis (status, action).
- Legend (pillar /25 | total /100): UGLY 5-10 | 20-40; BAD 11-15 | 41-60; GOOD 16-20 | 61-80; NINJA 21-25 | 81-100. Score where you are today; don't compare to others.
- Inputs: company name, date, completed by, Deal Flow Ladder phase (1-4), budget/situation profile -> personalize 30/60/90 plan.

**Expense tracker**: Monthly Revenue; Tools & Marketing (Prospectors, Skip Tracing, DataSift, Direct Mail, Bulk Dialer, Bulk SMS, Click-to-Call Tool); Other (Rent, Office, Utilities, Insurance, Software/Subscriptions, Phone/Internet); Team (Acquisitions, Lead Management, Leadership, Virtual Assistants: rate x # people). Outputs revenue, expenses, net profit, margin (0/20/50/100% scale). **Prospectors sit under Tools & Marketing on purpose**: tools + marketing + prospecting people / deals locked that month = fully loaded cost per deal; everyone else = team bucket.

**20 questions (1=Never, 5=Always)**
- Marketing: (1) systematic process to acquire + market new data; (2) easy to track what data is marketed + strategies; (3) multiple channels without duplicate targeting; (4) consistently generate leads for the specific problems we solve; (5) process to reach owners who ignored previous marketing.
- Sales Management: (1) new leads followed up + qualified quickly; (2) tasks completed on time by lead temperature; (3) repeatable handoff LM -> Acquisitions -> Transactions -> Dispositions; (4) all offers + seller communication documented in CRM; (5) leads/deals routinely audited for lost opportunity.
- Operations: (1) net profit margin: 1=0%, 2=1-20%, 3=21-40%, 4=41-50%, 5=>50%; (2) employees execute aligned with mission/vision/values; (3) all know roles + daily/weekly/monthly tasks; (4) know where SOPs are and use them; (5) flowcharts + process docs updated routinely.
- KPIs: (1) KPIs measure progress across business; (2) reviewed regularly by role; (3) data used to adjust strategy; (4) process to diagnose + fix nonperforming KPIs; (5) all team actions tracked + logged daily.
- Lowest pillar = biggest bottleneck, fix first. Marketing lowest -> stop everything, fix data + channels (no leads = nothing to manage).
- Do: one pillar at a time; re-audit every 90 days; verify 6 months sustainable; define Lifestyle vs Growth before KPI targets; log daily activity manually. Avoid: all four at once; inflating; skipping expense tracker; expecting revenue within 30 days (budget 90+ days to first deal); comparing to others. Can't sustain overhead 6 months without a deal -> bottleneck is cash flow.

**Doors per deal (DPD)** = live list size / deals produced; lower better. Elite <10 (precision), Strong 10-35 (precision), Volume >35 (bulk).
- Knox County, same six months: blind SFR 206; AI Investor Score 90+ 25.3; Absentee + Low Income 34.9; best ZIP 37914 104.9. Owner combos multiply: Absentee alone 105.9, Low Income alone 62.3, together 34.9. Distress stacked on AI score is worse: 48.4 vs 30.6 for AI score alone.
- Funnel stages: First to Market (obituary, pre-probate, probate) = pipeline builder, ~66 DPD, 4-7 month cycle, near-zero saturation; Niche Volume (senior, vacant, absentee stacks) = volume engine, faster/no deadline, low saturation, hard to reach; Deadline Distress (foreclosure, notice of default) = fastest, sharpest ratios, maxed saturation. Full read blends DPD, saturation, reach difficulty, cycle speed; run all three stages at once.
- Saturated lists (foreclosure) need a higher-quality touch — door knocking worked where calls didn't.

**Two-Channel System (fix marketing first)**
1. Both channels Day 1: 3-attempt call blitz over 72 hrs + Mail 01 handwritten letter ($1.75) start together; mail has no call prerequisite.
2. Cost ladder, not timeline: cost ranks spend freedom; cold call leads live contact; **text follows the call, never first**.
3. One system: Folders 01-08 = niche CALL/MAIL pairs; Folders 09-10 = bulk: 6 attempts across Priority 1, Priority 2, Tier 2. **FTM never enters bulk.**
4. Suppress, exhaust, re-enter: universal suppression in every preset; exhausted -> deep prospecting; timers bring back the rest.
- Reactivation (re-call people who said no, correct number only) vs Rehash (re-run never reached). Timers: 15 days foreclosure headed to auction; 45 days probate ("60 loses deals, 30 irritates"); 90 days all other FTM; 45 days Tier 2 not-interested. Don't skip — 20-30% of platform deals.

**Business type benchmarks**: Lifestyle $200-500K, 30-50%+ net, team 1-2 **[hiring guide: 50%+, 1-3]**; Growth $500K-5M+, 20% min, 5+. Realistic ceiling ~85%; Sam O'Neil ~92% solo, $800K/yr on ~$1,500/mo marketing.
**30/60/90 plan**: auto-generated from scores/phase/profile (73 tasks); all pillars 21+ -> maintain, quarterly re-audit, weekly KPI trend review, invest in next lever (team, market, channel). Don't look at 31-60 until 1-30 done.
**Quarterly Growth Engine**: (1) Audit — 20 questions, lowest pillar gets all energy 30 days; (2) Plan — 3 OKRs per quarter per division (5 max), yes/no or measurable, every task ladders up (25 contracts/yr -> 2 deals/mo min); Days 1-30 lowest pillar, 31-60 second-lowest, 61-90 systems for third; (3) Execute — 9-5 income-producing (calling, follow-up, closing), after 5 improvement projects (SOPs, dashboards); (4) Review — what works/doesn't, milestone gates hit, blockers, best news, rocks done or not, celebrate; (5) Re-audit — scores should rise each quarter; if not, rocks aimed wrong. Cash cycle 90+ days so quarterly re-audit coincides with first revenue.

## 6. Unit Economics

- Margin is the scorecard; example: $12M gross, $11M spend, ~$1M net. Model from businesses doing $2-3M/yr on ~$15K/mo marketing; 305+ success stories.
- Three levers: drive cost per contract down (efficient data); protect net margin (know fully loaded cost per deal); time every hire/spend to the cash conversion cycle.

**Fully loaded cost per deal** = (Marketing [mail, dials, every touch] + Data [lists, software] + Skip tracing + Callers [$750-1K each; prospecting labor = marketing, not team]) / deals LOCKED that month (signed contracts, not closings — closings lag). Run monthly.
- Industry average $7K-15K per contract (approx.) = benchmark to beat.
- Cost per contract: FTM solo $500-1K; FTM with team/VAs $1K-2K; broad AI alone (no cheaper tiers under) ~$4K-5K (approx., varies).
- (Phase 4 page formula differs: Marketing + Data + Team (every seat) / deals closed.) **[conflict]**

**Tier stacking (sequence, not menu)**: lead with SiftMap precision (pull + market same day; slightly higher cost per contract, faster cash) -> deals fund courthouse build -> FTM underneath (cheapest once built $500-2,000; county filings take 25-35 days to reach paid brokers; build takes 25-35 days) -> profit rolls into broad AI. Blended cost per contract drops.
- FTM alone captures 10-30% of an area's market-share deals; all tiers stacked ~80%.
- Trestle phone scoring: cuts ~50% of numbers while reaching ~80% of correct ones.
- Pace check: 75-100 correct-number not-interesteds ≈ 1 deal over 12 months.

**Margin by phase**: best solo 92% (Sam O'Neil ~$800K on ~$1,500/mo, ~$21K total spend); healthy 30-50%+, mastered up to ~85%. P1 you are the labor, highest ceiling; P2 callers/data roll into cost per deal, number rises; P3 sales layer joins team bucket; P4 gross grows fastest, so does the trap.
- Keep all-in tech stack < $2,000/mo plus people; when margin slips, audit software creep before cutting marketing.

**4 gates before raising marketing spend**
1. Current tier producing — validated, locking deals at/under target cost per contract.
2. Can float cash cycle — wholesale: money back in ~3-4 months (30-45 days to contract + 30-60 to close); flips 7-9 months.
3. Runway banked — 6-12 months operating costs in business + 3-6 months personal (~$30-50K) beside it.
4. Raise is cash, not debt — fund from closed-deal revenue; never borrow to market.
- Runway rule: $10K/mo all-in operating -> ~$60K in bank before hiring.
- Hiring gate: personally close ~$100K first (~10 wholesale deals); stay the closer as long as feasible.
- New hire takes ~90 days to drive revenue; plus 3-4 month cash cycle -> may be two quarters before net-positive.

**Monthly two buckets**: Bucket 1 fully loaded cost per deal (software + data + mail + callers/prospectors / deals locked); Bucket 2 general team (acquisitions, LM, leadership, other VAs). Then self-score 4 pillars 1-5, fix lowest; revisit monthly, full re-audit quarterly.

## 7. Phase 1: The Operator (Solo + Claude)

- You are every seat (data, dials, follow-up, closing). Off your plate: nothing — by design; you can't delegate a process you've never run.
- First hire = data manager $500-700/mo only after closed deals fund it, with 3-6 months of costs banked.
- Deal Flow Ladder: 4 phases, DPD decides list, hires, phase. Climb on overflow only; never skip a rung. Bands Elite <10, Strong 10-35, Volume >35; elite lists lead, volume = coverage.
- Pick lowest-DPD list that still captures most market share: Judgments 6.2; Pre-foreclosure Final Judgement 9.4; Negative Equity 10.9; Estate Sales 12.4; Pre-Probate/Deceased 14.8; Investor AI Score 90+ ~22 (run as parallel precision pull, NOT stacked on the niche list).
- Sensitive lists: deceased/pre-probate — mail family immediately, no calls until 3 months after filing, never a cold text. Only ~30% of deceased owners ever file probate.
- Buy box example: $100-700K; single-family off MLS; equity 30%+ or unknown. Set before pulling a record.
- One market: DPD picks it, Market Finder confirms; widen only after flow maxed.

**Six moves (in order)**
1. Pick one market (DPD data), commit, no second market.
2. Pull most efficient niche list; apply buy box before any skip trace.
3. Stand up sequential flow: skip trace, phone scoring, click-to-dial, call-attempt counter on every record.
4. Safety nets: sold suppression, auto-add for new matching records, number reputation monitoring.
5. Choose run mode.
6. Seed First-to-Market with Claude day one (long sales cycle).
- Dial one line at a time from the record, counter in view; click-to-dial (smrtPhone) keeps 150/day.

**Three modes**: You dial ($0.03-0.06/call; 4 full attempts: call every number, VM, text, bump counter by hand; work not-interested queue daily); A caller dials ($750-1,000/mo from day one, 150/day; you keep follow-up, appts, offers); Mail only/lifestyle ($0.50-0.75/touch; steady cadence, best leads call you).
- 75-100 correct-number not-interesteds ≈ 1 deal in 12 months.
- One engine two speeds: SiftMap niche list = fast (conversations this week, contracts this quarter); FTM county data = slow, seeded by Claude day one. Skip slow cycle -> Phase 2 opens with nothing maturing.

**Graduation gates (all four)**: flow live (skip trace, scoring, click-to-dial, counters, safety nets); daily dials, every record gets 4 full attempts before leaving queue; FTM seeded (Claude pulls on schedule); leads exceed what you can hold, follow-ups slipping (trigger, not failure). Overflow funds first hire.

## 8. Phase 2: The Delegator

- Off your plate: data + admin. Keep every seller conversation (learn the market you'll later manage).
- **Data Manager** $500-700/mo replaces list pulls, skip tracing, CRM hygiene: pulls niche lists + skip traces; loads, tags, dedupes; preps mail files for no-number and non-responder lists; keeps tomorrow's call list full.
- Still yours: every call attempt (or run mail yourself); every offer + negotiation; set/enforce daily order; log objections your first caller will train on.
- **Do not hire a caller yet** — overflow not earned; your conversations become the Phase 3 caller's script.
- Add touches, not markets: 4 full attempts per record; 1 market until overflow.
- Cost per touch: SMS $0.01; calling $0.03-0.06; mail $0.50-0.75; deep prospecting $1.50-4.00; door knocking $25-50. Cheap touches on everyone, expensive only where nothing else reaches.

**Daily call order (never double-dial; closest to deal first, never-called last)**
1. Follow-ups + rehash (scheduled callbacks + not-interested list), before any cold dial.
2. Attempt 3 records (4th and final attempt today — convert or move to mail).
3. Attempt 2 records (3rd attempt; call every number again).
4. Attempt 1 records (2nd attempt; VM + personalized text again).
5. Ready to call (fresh records last).
- One full attempt = call every number, leave VM, send personalized text, increment attempt counter manually. After 4 -> mail list.

**Mail**: second wave, except skip-traced zero-number list gets mail FIRST (no investor can call them; $0.50-0.75/piece). Others earn mail after 4th attempt. Alternate mailer type every send. Text stays attached to calls (Smarter Contact, $0.01/message).
**Obituary rules**: mail family immediately (only first touch allowed); first call only when probate filing ≥3 months old (set date in CRM); never cold text any sensitive list. Obituary reaches the ~70% who never file probate.
**Handoff numbers**: track dials, answer rate, correct numbers, leads daily; 150 dials/day benchmark; 4 attempts; 75-100 not-interesteds per deal. Correct-number not-interested = inventory: log, drip, call back, work daily.
- Case studies: Kasim Delake ($100K+ wholesaling on county niche data with full-time W-2, one market, lean data help); Josh ($60K deal from abandoned liens list, zero new records, added attempts).
- Graduate on overflow, never ambition/revenue target/date/boredom. Slip signals: follow-ups sliding a day; new leads waiting for first call. Bank 3-6 months of next hire's cost, then move.
- Next steps: Direct Mail Mastery; drip sequences so not-interested list works itself daily.

## 9. Phase 3: The Manager

- One trade: phones leave your plate; a system replaces your memory. Off plate: callers dial 150/day each; LM qualifies every answer and works not-interested daily; first touch -> qualified lead without you. Still yours: read the board every morning, coach callers on real calls, take qualified sellers to contract, hiring.

- 2 callers per LM; 150 dials/caller/day. Seat costs: DM $500-700; Caller $750-1,000; LM $1,500-3,000; Closer $2,000 + 3-10%; Sales Mgr (P4) $5-6K + 2-5%.

**Money Gate**: busy is not a trigger; overflow triggers each seat, 3-6 months of the seat's cost banked before posting the job; revenue funds monthly. Caller -> $2,250-6,000 banked before day one; LM -> $4,500-18,000. No runway, no hire. One seat at a time.

**System before people (build before first interview)**: Statuses (one status per lead telling the next person the exact next action); Tasks (every follow-up a dated task); Boards (one view of every lead by stage; nothing in notebook/head); Structured intake (same fields every new lead); Default cadences (4 full attempts: every number, VM, text, counter); not-interested list worked daily. See Lead Management + Lead Intake Custom Fields guides.

**Fixed hiring order**: (1) Admin/DM $500-700 (pulls, skip trace, dedupe, clean intake; one admin running Claude covers several); (2) Prospectors $750-1,000 each, start 1-2, 150 floor from week one, **two weeks under the floor = coach or replace**; (3) LM $1,500-3,000 (qualifies, works not-interested, owns board; hire when caller output outruns your follow-up); (4) Closer $2,000 + 3-10% (only after first three seats produce more appointments than you can run). One admin + 1-2 prospectors before any salesperson. One rubric per candidate; written SOP per seat.

## 10. Phase 4: The Owner

- Last thing off your plate: the deals. Leadership = rung 4. Two hires: **Sales Manager** $5-6K + 2-5% override (owns closers, call reviews, sales scorecard); **Ops Manager** $5-7K + bonus (systems, reporting, hiring pipeline). - Deep prospecting $1.50-4.00/record: heirs, executors, dead numbers, return mail. Hardest-to-reach = least competed, highest margin; yours is the only offer.

**Instrument everything**: KPIs, call coaching, scorecards flow automatically on schedule. Floor numbers 150 dials, 4 attempts, 75-100 not-interesteds/deal; not-interesteds worked daily, not archived.
